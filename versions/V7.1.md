# V7.1 — Land Expansion

New cities start in A4, a 16×10 plot around the original highway. Buy neighboring plots to grow across the sixteen-plot 64×40 region. Expand city highlights ownership and available plots; a 4×4 button grid makes selection accessible on phones. The purchase button shows the exact price and cannot buy an owned, disconnected or unaffordable plot.

Base cost starts at $5,000 and grows by $1,500 for each expansion already bought. Water coverage adds a small terrain adjustment; prices round to $250. Purchases charge the treasury once, unlock construction immediately and persist in browser/JSON saves. Free build makes claims free, including when the treasury is negative, while preserving adjacency rules.

Unowned land rejects roads, zones, rail, demolition, district painting and map edits. Inspection remains available. Apply or cancel map editing before buying land. Older V4–V7 saves receive the complete region so existing cities stay usable; Showcase and all fifteen ready-made designs also remain fully unlocked.

The region remains 64×40 tiles; this update adds a land-ownership progression within that region. All twenty-five Node tests pass, with Chromium desktop/mobile checks for paid purchases, free claims, construction boundaries and ownership persistence.
