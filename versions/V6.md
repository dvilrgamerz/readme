# V6 — Traffic & Economy

## Playable additions

- **64×40 regional map:** 2,560 tiles, about 78% more space than V5's 1,440 tiles. Existing 48×30 cities occupy the same coordinates and are surrounded by new land.
- **Traffic queues:** vehicles keep following gaps, wait before red signals, and yield when the next road segment is occupied. Junctions have alternating signal phases by default; the Traffic Lights tool toggles them.
- **Custom buses:** build connected bus stops, select New line, tap 2–16 stops in order, and save a closed loop. Up to 12 lines and 4 buses per line; each bus costs $18/day. Lines serve nearby homes and workplaces and reduce commuter car demand. Buses pause briefly at stops.
- **Factory → truck → shop:** staffed, powered industry produces goods each day. Physical trucks reserve factory goods and deliver them to shops. Shops consume stock and earn reduced tax revenue when empty. Breaking a delivery route returns reserved goods to its factory.
- **Garbage:** occupied zones produce waste daily. A connected garbage depot dispatches up to three trucks. Waste is removed when a truck arrives, or immediately when the depot and property share the same road access. Uncollected waste lowers health, land value, and growth.
- **Households and commuting:** residential properties contain household groups, workers, and students. Workers are assigned to reachable jobs; the inspector shows employment, education, and commute distance. Household counts are aggregated per property, rather than rendering every person as a separate agent.
- **Building controls:** the inspector lists development blockers and offers paid level upgrades and density conversion. High-density zoning costs twice as much and supports 2.5× capacity.
- **Budget:** residential, commercial, and industrial taxes; transit fares; roads; property upkeep; services; bus operations; policies; and debt repayment. Displayed line items sum exactly to the projected daily net.
- **Save migration:** V4/V5 maps expand into V6 without moving old tiles or discarding funds, levels, or districts. V6 backups preserve inventories, routes, signals, and in-flight deliveries. Household assignment data is recalculated when loaded.

## Controls

- Bus route: **New line → tap bus stops → Save line**. On phones, use Map while selecting stops, then return to Build & manage.
- Signals: **Traffic Lights → tap a three- or four-way junction**.
- Buildings: **Inspect → select property → upgrade level or convert density**.
- Zoom reaches 500% for comfortable phone interaction with the larger region.

## Simulation limits

Traffic follows shortest road routes with grid-based lane spacing and simplified junction occupancy. Congestion is measured from waiting simulated vehicles; fleets are capped at 180 for browser performance. Household simulation uses property-level groups. Shops have one generic goods inventory, waste is a simplified per-property value, and garbage pickup does not simulate a landfill chain. Bus ridership is estimated from route coverage and worker demand; pedestrians are not individually animated. Metro remains a capacity service. The renderer remains Canvas 2D.

## Validation

`npm test` checks runtime startup, access, utilities, save/load, migration, routing, service coverage, signal queues, following distances, frame-rate independence, truck inventory conservation, route refunds, garbage arrivals, bus route reachability, household accounting, budget sums and V6 save validation.

GitHub Actions also runs a Chromium playtest at desktop and phone sizes and uploads screenshots as `metroforge-v6-browser-qa`. The game is deployed from `main` only after validation passes and Pages is enabled.
