# V7 — City Playground

Free-build mode and fifteen deterministic, fully populated city designs make testing city planning quick. Every layout includes connected roads, sufficient power/water, jobs, services, a bus loop and rail corridors. Load replaces the current city after a confirmation; use Save or Export first.

## Gameplay

- Passenger stations connect automatically along painted rail. Two stations, road access, homes and workplaces near stations are needed for ridership.
- Cargo shuttles reserve up to 60 factory goods and deliver them on arrival. Terminals serve properties within seven tiles; delivered goods reduce demand for trucks.
- Hotels hold 80 visitors each. Attractions and passenger rail create demand; happiness and pollution affect occupancy. Visitors contribute $2/day each.
- Office zones provide clean jobs, with education of 45% required for growth and employees. Office taxes use the commercial rate.
- Fire engines and ambulances queue on roads. Fires reset a building's development if no crew arrives within 90 simulation seconds; medical timeouts increase distress. Test incidents from City report.
- Edit custom maps with free terrain/road painting. Apply keeps the edits with the original treasury; Cancel restores the original city. Save/export during editing backs up the original until Apply.
- Three challenges award $5,000–7,000 after five consecutive qualifying days. Rewards are once per challenge and disabled during free build.
- Bus names/stops can be edited, stops undone, and fleets reduced. Opposing traffic uses separate lanes when entering the next road segment.

## Free build

Construction, demolition and upgrades cost nothing. Treasury/debt settlement and random disasters pause; zoning, traffic, utilities, deliveries and test incidents still simulate. Turn it off to resume normal finances. Your choice persists in saves and when loading a design.

## Validation and compatibility

21 Node tests cover road access, utility supply, all city designs, free costs, editor transactions, goods conservation, office education, tourism, emergency arrivals/timeouts and challenge rewards. Chromium CI checks desktop and mobile controls, every design, persistence, gameplay, layout and active-city frame timing, saving screenshots as workflow artifacts.

V4–V6 saves migrate. Train positions are reconstructed after load and reserved cargo is refunded in the saved snapshot; pending incidents redispatch road crews. Tracks are an overlay independent of buildings and roads. Rail uses automatic shuttles and simplified terminal transfers, rather than railway scheduling or individual passengers.
