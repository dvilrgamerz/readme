# MetroForge Version History

## V2 — City Systems Expansion
V2 turns the original prototype into a deeper management game.

### Added
- Land value simulation
- Pollution simulation
- Health and education systems
- Crime and police coverage
- Service-budget control
- Municipal loans
- Green, transit, and education policies
- Better zoning growth logic
- Utility shortages affecting growth
- Improved alerts and economy balancing

## V3 — Roads, Traffic & Transit
V3 focuses on connectivity and transportation.

### Added
- Connected road network simulation
- Zones require access to the connected road network
- Two road classes: roads and avenues
- Animated traffic vehicles
- Traffic congestion calculation
- Bus stops and metro stations
- Transit ridership
- Free-transit policy
- Traffic overlay
- Improved job/population demand model

## V4 — World Simulation
V4 is the current live build.

### Added
- Procedurally generated maps
- River terrain
- Day/night cycle
- Dynamic weather: clear, cloudy, rain, storm, heatwave
- Animated rain
- Fire and flood disaster events
- District painting
- Land-value overlay
- Pollution overlay
- Service overlay
- Missions with rewards
- Expanded milestones
- Hospitals and recycling centers
- Advanced happiness calculation
- City health, education, crime and pollution metrics
- Automatic browser saves
- Manual save/load
- Responsive desktop/mobile interface
- Larger 48×30 map
- Zoom support
- Expanded economy and debt system
